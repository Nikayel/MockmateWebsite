## Executive Summary

This document outlines CodeSparring's comprehensive SEO strategy, including keyword research, content calendar, technical SEO requirements, and backlink acquisition strategy. The goal is to establish organic dominance in the "coding interview preparation" space.

## 1. Target Audience & Search Intent Analysis

### Primary Audience Segments

| Segment | Description | Search Intent | Content Needs |
|---------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| CS Students | Preparing for first internship/job | Informational + Transactional | Beginner guides, patterns, company prep |
| Career Changers | Bootcamp grads, self-taught devs | Informational + Commercial | Transition guides, study plans |
| Experienced Devs | FAANG-bound engineers | Transactional + Commercial | Advanced patterns, system design, specific company prep |
| International Devs | H1B visa seekers | Informational | US interview culture, visa-specific prep |

### Search Intent Breakdown

- **Informational (60%):** "what is two pointers pattern", "how to prepare for coding interview"
- **Commercial Investigation (25%):** "best coding interview prep platform", "leetcode vs codesparring"
- **Transactional (15%):** "coding interview practice with AI", "mock interview platform"

## 8. Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunity |
|------------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| LeetCode | Brand recognition, problem library | No AI interviewer, expensive | Position as "practice partner" |
| Pramp | Free peer practice | No AI, scheduling friction | 24/7 AI availability |
| Interviewing.io | Real engineers | Expensive, limited availability | Affordable AI alternative |
| AlgoExpert | Great explanations | Static content, no practice | Interactive AI feedback |
| Exponent | System design focus | Limited coding practice | Comprehensive coverage |

### Content Gap Analysis
- Most competitors lack: spaced repetition science, psychology content, voice-enabled practice
- Opportunity: Own "science of interview prep" narrative
- Differentiation: AI interviewer available 24/7, forgetting curve-based scheduling
