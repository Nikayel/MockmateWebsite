# MockMate Pricing

Subscription plans and usage limits.

## Plans

### Free Tier

**Price**: $0/month

**Includes**:
- 3 interview simulations per month
- Basic coding challenges
- AI interviewer feedback
- Performance tracking
- VS Code integration

**Perfect for**: Trying out MockMate, occasional practice

### Pro Tier

**Price**: $19/month

**Includes**:
- Unlimited interview sessions
- Advanced coding challenges
- System design interviews
- Detailed analytics & insights
- Priority support
- Custom interview scenarios
- Custom difficulty levels

**Perfect for**: Serious interview preparation, regular practice

### Enterprise

**Price**: Custom

**Includes**:
- Everything in Pro
- Team management
- Custom interview templates
- SSO integration
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated support

**Perfect for**: Companies, teams, bootcamps

## Usage Limits

### Free Tier
- **Simulations**: 3 per month
- Resets on the 1st of each month
- Usage tracked automatically

### Pro Tier
- **Simulations**: Effectively unlimited (30+ per month)
- No monthly reset needed
- All features unlocked

## Billing

- **Payment**: Credit card via Stripe (coming soon)
- **Billing Cycle**: Monthly
- **Cancellation**: Cancel anytime
- **Refunds**: 30-day money-back guarantee

## Upgrading

1. Go to `/upgrade` on the website
2. Click "Upgrade to Pro"
3. Complete payment
4. Your extension immediately gets Pro access

## Downgrading

- Cancel anytime from your account page
- Access continues until end of billing period
- Automatically downgrades to Free tier

## FAQ

**Q: What happens if I reach my free limit?**
A: You'll see a message prompting you to upgrade. You can wait for the monthly reset or upgrade to Pro.

**Q: Can I upgrade mid-month?**
A: Yes! You'll get immediate Pro access and your usage resets.

**Q: Do unused simulations roll over?**
A: No, free tier resets monthly. Pro tier has no limits.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit cards via Stripe (coming soon).
